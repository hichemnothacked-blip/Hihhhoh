Who we are

UXClaim ("we", "us") is a team of developers, video editors, marketers and designers. Contact details: add your email and postal address here.

What we collect

  • Account details: name, email address, roles and a short bio.
  • Work you publish: titles, descriptions, links and cover images.
  • Messages you send through our forms.
  • Basic usage data: pages visited and device type.

How we use it

To run accounts, show the work members publish, answer your messages, keep the site secure and improve it.

Sharing

Work you publish is shown publicly under your name. We do not sell personal data. Providers that host the site or send email process data on our behalf under agreements that protect it.

Cookies and storage

We use essential storage to keep you signed in and remember preferences such as theme. Analytics, if added, will ask for your consent first.

How long we keep it

While your account is active. When you delete your account we remove your profile and published work, except where the law requires us to keep records.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, and withdraw consent at any time. Write to us at the address above.

Security

We use access controls and encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and tell account holders about material changes.
